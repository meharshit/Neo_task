The glossary defines the terms used across the Tasket docs. Terms on other pages show a short definition when you hold the pointer over them, and link back to this page.

## Admin

A team role with every permission that members have, plus actions that affect a whole project. Only admins can turn off Tracks.

## Assignee

A person responsible for a task. A task can have up to 10 assignees, and every assigned task appears in the assignee's **My Work**.

## Closed task

A task whose task status is Completed or Discarded. A closed task keeps its track statuses, and you can reopen it to return it to Open.

## Friday

Neo's AI assistant, co-worker, and agent platform, built into Tasket, Studio, and Drive. Every task has its own Friday chat, a conversation with Friday about that task.

## Guest

A person from outside your organization who's part of a team. On the projects they can access, guests have the same Tracks permissions as members.

## Member

A person from your organization who's part of a team. Members can turn on Tracks, set up tracks, and move tasks through them.

## My Work

A list of every task you're assigned to.

## Project

A collection of tasks inside a team. Every task belongs to exactly one project, and each project has its own tracks.

## Studio

Neo's app for docs, spreadsheets, and diagrams. You can attach Studio documents to a task.

## Task

A single unit of work in Tasket. A task has a title, a comment thread, attached Studio documents, and a Friday chat. It has one creator, up to 10 assignees, and any number of watchers.

## Task status

Where a task stands overall: Open, Completed, or Discarded. A task's status doesn't change its track statuses, and its track statuses don't change its task status.

## Team

A group of people who work together in Tasket, made up of people from inside your organization and guests from outside it. Each team has members, admins, and guests, and contains projects.

## Track

A workstream in a project, such as Spec, Design, or QA. Each track appears as a column on the Tracks board and exists only in the project where it was created.

## Track status

Where a task stands on one track: Not started, In Progress, or Done. A task has one track status for each track in its project.

## Tracks board

The **Tracks** tab of a project. It shows one column for each track, and each column has an **In Progress** section and a **Done** section.

## Tracks pill

The control in a task's detail view that lists every track in the project and the task's track status on each one.

## Updates

A list of activity on the tasks you take part in.

## Watcher

A person who follows a task's activity without being assigned to it. A task can have any number of watchers.
